Why DAFPIE?

Food waste and hunger are two major social problems. We solve both with one simple platform.

Reduce Waste

Thousands of tons of perfectly good food go to waste daily. We help divert it to those who need it most.

Simple Platform

A digital bridge connecting food donors, NGOs, and volunteer delivery partners in real-time.

Feed the Hungry

Empowering NGOs to focus on distribution while we handle the sourcing and logistics.

How It Works

Step 1: Donate

Donor adds details about surplus food.

Step 2: Match

NGO finds suitable available food nearby.

Step 3: Pickup

Volunteer collects the food from the donor.

Step 4: Deliver

Food reaches the NGO or people in need.
