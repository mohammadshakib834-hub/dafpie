# Problem Statement

Food waste and hunger are two simultaneous crises. On one hand, restaurants, event organizers, and households waste millions of tons of edible food every year. On the other hand, a large population goes to bed hungry.

There is a significant gap between food surplus and food deficit. The existing manual processes of identifying surplus food, contacting NGOs, and arranging logistics are inefficient, slow, and uncoordinated. Food often perishes before it can be delivered to those in need.

# Objectives

The objective of DAFPIE (Donate And Feed People In Need) is to bridge this gap by providing a seamless, real-time digital platform. 

1. **Reduce Food Waste**: Provide an easy way for donors to report surplus food.
2. **Empower NGOs**: Give NGOs a centralized dashboard to find and claim available food in their vicinity.
3. **Streamline Logistics**: Create a gig-style volunteer network to handle the pickup and delivery of food from the donor to the NGO.
4. **Smart Optimization**: Use algorithms (Urgency Score, Smart Matching) to prioritize highly perishable food and match it with the nearest capable NGO.
5. **Transparency**: Track the complete lifecycle of a food donation (Available -> Accepted -> Picked Up -> On The Way -> Delivered).
